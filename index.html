https://diskominfobdgjabar.github.io/account-deletion-policy/

The dedicated account deletion page sets out the current request methods and detailed instructions. At the time of this Privacy Policy, a user may request account deletion through the in-application account deletion feature, where available, or by following the instructions on the dedicated account deletion page above.

To help us process a deletion request securely and accurately, the request should include sufficient information to identify and verify the account holder, including where applicable:

  • the full name of the account holder;
  • the application name, namely BWM Bandung Operator;
  • the account identifier, username, phone number, or other registered account details used in the Application;
  • the email address or other contact information associated with the account; and
  • a clear statement requesting the permanent deletion of the account and related personal data.

After a valid request is received, we may take reasonable steps to verify the identity of the requester before processing the deletion. Deletion of an account may result in the permanent loss of access to the Application, user content, records, preferences, and other data linked to the account. Once the deletion process has been completed, such data may not be restored.

Certain limited data may still be retained after account deletion where required or permitted by law, including non-personally identifiable security logs retained for up to 90 days and anonymized statistical information used for analysis and service improvement.

If you require assistance regarding this account deletion procedure, you may contact us at android@diskominfo.bandung.go.id.
